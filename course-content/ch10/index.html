Chapter 10 · Helm & Kustomize in pipelines

Contoso now deploys the same app to dev and prod with small differences: replicas, image tag, limits. Copying YAML per environment drifts. This chapter builds k8s/base plus k8s/overlays/{dev,prod} with Kustomize, packages the app as a Helm chart, and wires both into the pipeline so one image is promoted everywhere.

By the end, you can

  • Structure manifests as a Kustomize base with dev/prod overlays (patches, images, replicas, namespace).
  • Read and write a small Helm chart: Chart.yaml, values.yaml, templates, helpers.
  • Install and upgrade releases safely, and roll back.
  • Store charts as OCI artifacts in ACR.
  • Wire either approach into azure-pipelines.yml, with one artifact promoted across environments.
