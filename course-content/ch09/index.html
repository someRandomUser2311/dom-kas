Chapter 9 · Network policies: default-deny & zero trust

Every pod in Contoso's cluster can currently talk to every other pod, including the database. This chapter locks that down: deny by default, then allow exactly the flows the app needs. These files become k8s/policies/.

By the end, you can

  • Explain the default (everything can talk to everything), and what makes a pod "isolated" for ingress or egress.
  • Write default-deny policies, plus precise allow rules using podSelector, namespaceSelector and ports.
  • Avoid the classic traps: AND vs OR selectors, forgetting DNS egress, and policies being additive.
  • Choose a policy engine on AKS, and know when L7 or FQDN policies (Cilium) are needed.
