Azure Kubernetes Service · ~2.5 h

Chapter 5 · AKS networking & identity

Two topics decide most AKS designs: how traffic flows (pod IPs, load balancers, egress) and who is allowed to do what (cluster, kubelet, humans, and apps). By the end, orders-api can read from Azure services with no secrets at all.

By the end, you can

  • Choose between Azure CNI Overlay, Azure CNI with VNet-routed pod IPs and Azure CNI powered by Cilium, and explain why kubenet is going away.
  • Plan IP address space for nodes, pods and services.
  • Explain how a LoadBalancer Service becomes an Azure Load Balancer, and make it internal.
  • Pick an outbound type: load balancer, NAT gateway, or user-defined routing through a firewall.
  • Distinguish the cluster identity, the kubelet identity and workload identities.
  • Set up Entra ID authentication with Azure RBAC for Kubernetes authorization.
  • Configure workload identity end to end.
