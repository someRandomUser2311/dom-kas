Chapter 14 · Observability & troubleshooting

Something is broken in production. This chapter teaches a calm, repeatable way to find out what, built almost entirely from real diagnostic output. Then it sets up the AKS monitoring that tells you before your users do.

By the end, you can

  • Follow a systematic triage path: get → describe → events → logs → exec/debug.
  • Recognise and fix: Pending, ImagePullBackOff, CrashLoopBackOff, OOMKilled, readiness failing, and a Service with no endpoints.
  • Use AKS observability: Container Insights, Azure Monitor managed Prometheus, Managed Grafana, and control-plane diagnostic logs.
  • Write useful alerts, and read a failed pipeline deploy log.
