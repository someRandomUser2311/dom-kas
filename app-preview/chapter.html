Azure Pipelines: CI/CD to AKS

Learn how a commit becomes a running pod. You'll read, write and debug real azure-pipelines.yml files, wire up service connections and ACR, design multi-stage pipelines with approvals, and ship an app to AKS by hand so you know exactly what the pipeline automates.

  • Design stages with conditions, environments & approvals
  • Use variables, secrets & templates correctly
